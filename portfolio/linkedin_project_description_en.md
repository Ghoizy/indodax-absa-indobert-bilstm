**Project title**
Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis of Indodax Reviews | IndoBERT + BiLSTM

**Project description (LinkedIn Projects section)**
Designed and evaluated a hybrid IndoBERT–BiLSTM pipeline for Indonesian aspect-based sentiment analysis on cryptocurrency exchange app reviews. Processed 80,000 Google Play reviews into a 68,204-review corpus; coordinated a three-annotator labeling workflow; applied near-duplicate-aware splitting and training-only oversampling; and evaluated aspect, sentiment, conditional pipeline, and joint ABSA predictions. Achieved 90.19% test Macro F1 for aspect classification and generated aspect-level insights from the full corpus. Research notebooks, methodology, and visualizations are documented on GitHub.
